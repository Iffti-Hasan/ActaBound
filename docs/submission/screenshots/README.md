# ActaBound: Recommended Screenshots Guide

This folder (`docs/submission/screenshots/`) is where you can save screenshots of your working ActaBound dashboard.

Although our HTML presentation deck, whitepaper, and reports are already **100% complete, fully styled, and self-contained with built-in diagrams and visual metric cards**, capturing these 6 clean screenshots will make your submission look exceptionally authentic and impressive to the judges!

---

## The 6 Recommended Screenshots

### 1. `01_overview_command_center.png`
- **Where to go:** `http://localhost:3000` (Overview page)
- **What to capture:** The hero banner, Total Portfolio Value ($1,670.00), Protected Reserve ($200.00), Remaining Daily Budget ($150.00), and Attack Arena Security Pass (18/18 100%).
- **Why judges love it:** Shows an enterprise, polished fintech dashboard with high contrast in both light and dark modes.

### 2. `02_portfolio_interactive_charts.png`
- **Where to go:** `http://localhost:3000/portfolio`
- **What to capture:** The Asset Allocation Donut Chart (USDC 29.9% vs ETH 70.1%) and the Liquidity & Encumbrance bar chart.
- **Why judges love it:** Visually proves that assets are categorized into spendable operating funds vs locked reserves.

### 3. `03_ai_agent_permitted_transfer.png`
- **Where to go:** `http://localhost:3000/chat` (AI Command Center)
- **What to capture:** Click the quick scenario button `"Permitted Transfer (40 USDC)"` and hit Send. Capture the green `ALLOW (PERMITTED_WITHIN_MANDATE)` badge and transaction receipt.
- **Why judges love it:** Proves the autonomous agent interprets natural language into structured plans and executes permitted operations.

### 4. `04_ai_agent_blocked_attack.png`
- **Where to go:** `http://localhost:3000/chat`
- **What to capture:** Click the quick scenario `"Prohibited: Reserve Breach (350 USDC)"` or `"Attack: Prompt Injection Bypass"` and hit Send. Capture the red `BLOCK (RESERVE_BREACH)` card.
- **Why judges love it:** Fulfills Topic F's mandatory requirement: proving prohibited actions are blocked fail-closed prior to signing or broadcasting.

### 5. `05_financial_twin_simulation.png`
- **Where to go:** `http://localhost:3000/twin`
- **What to capture:** The interactive bar chart with the **$200.00 Reserve Floor reference line**, before vs projected after state cards, and the alternative transfer recommendation.
- **Why judges love it:** Demonstrates pre-flight risk forecasting and explainable AI in decentralized finance.

### 6. `06_autonomous_attack_arena_18_passed.png`
- **Where to go:** `http://localhost:3000/arena`
- **What to capture:** Click `"Run All 18 Scenarios"`. Capture the green **18 / 18 (100%)** badge showing all adversarial scenarios defended.
- **Why judges love it:** Proves your security isn't just theory—it has withstood 18 automated red-team security attacks.

## How to Take & Save:
1. Open Chrome or Edge at `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Press `Windows Key + Shift + S` (Windows Snipping Tool) to capture each region.
3. Save the image into this folder: `D:\FinTechathon\docs\submission\screenshots\`.
